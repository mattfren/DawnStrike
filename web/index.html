Evidence is allowed to be incomplete.

A missing close, unknown cost, or unresolved observation stays out of the realized total. That can make the dashboard look less polished. It makes the result more trustworthy.

Forward evidence takes time.Strategy quality is not marked proven by a short sample or a backtest alone.
